DANISH JALBANI — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

General Questions
------------------

Q: Who is Danish Jalbani?

high-quality custom WordPress websites and helping businesses establish a strong
digital presence.

Q: What makes Danish different from other web developers?
A: Danish is "More Than a Web Developer." He combines technical development skills
with entrepreneurial experience and business consulting. He doesn't just build
websites — he helps clients think about their online presence strategically. With
15+ years of experience across multiple countries and industries, he brings a

Q: What countries do you work with?
A: Danish works with clients globally. He has completed projects for clients in
USA, UK, UAE, Australia, Pakistan, and other countries. All communication and

Q: What platforms do you specialize in?
A: WordPress is the primary platform. Danish also works with Shopify, Webflow,
and WooCommerce. For design-to-development, he converts Figma and Adobe XD
designs into fully functional websites.

A: Yes. If you have a design already created in Figma, Adobe XD, or as a PSD file,

Q: Do you offer business consulting beyond website development?

Pricing & Process Questions
-----------------------------

Q: How much does a website cost?
A: Pricing is customized based on the project's scope, complexity, and requirements.
To get an accurate quote, please get in touch through danishjalbani.com on contact form or any
of Danish's social media channels. Pricing varies based on complexity of the project. 
Pre-built designed websites start from $500, while custom build starts from $800.
Ecommerce websites start from $1000.

Q: How do I get started?
A: Simply reach out through the contact form at danishjalbani.com or send a
message on Facebook, LinkedIn, or Instagram. Share details about your project
and Danish will get back to you to discuss the next steps.

Q: How long does a website project take?
A: Project timelines vary based on complexity and requirements. This will be
discussed and agreed upon before the project begins. Simple websites with a pre-built design 
can take from 5-7 days, while custom design builds can take from 2-3 weeks.

Q: What is Your Revision Policy?
A: Depending on the project, client may get 5 revisions per project to more than 10.
In projects that are custom designed and have big budgets, client's get unlimited revisions.
Danish believe's in forming a long term relationship and providing support to the business in 
order to provide value.

Q: What info do you require to get started?
A: Client name, business name and contact details. Kind of project you are looking for.
Budget for the project if any, designed references, if any. Any other detail client would like
to share, please reach out through contact form on the website.
