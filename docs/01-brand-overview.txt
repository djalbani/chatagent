DANISH JALBANI — BRAND OVERVIEW
================================

Who Is Danish Jalbani?
-----------------------
Danish Jalbani is a Pakistani freelance web developer, entrepreneur, motivational speaker, and online business consultant with 15+ years of experience. He is based in Pakistan and serves clients globally including USA, UK, UAE, Australia, and other countries.

His tagline is: "More Than a Web Developer"

Danish is not just a developer — he combines deep technical expertise with entrepreneurial thinking and business consulting. He helps businesses not just build websites, but establish powerful digital presences that drive real results.

Mission & Values
-----------------
Danish's mission is to help businesses and individuals build a strong online presence that reflects their brand, attracts their target audience, and generates real business outcomes.

He believes in:
- Delivering quality over quantity
- Building long-term client relationships, not just one-time projects
- Combining design, development, and strategy together
- Empowering clients with knowledge, not keeping them dependent
