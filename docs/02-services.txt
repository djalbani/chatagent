DANISH JALBANI — SERVICES
==========================

Core Services
--------------

1. CUSTOM WORDPRESS WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT
   Danish builds fully custom WordPress websites from scratch. This includes custom themes,
   Elementor Pro page building, responsive design for all devices, SEO-ready structure,
   speed optimization, and security setup. No cookie-cutter templates — every site is
   built specifically for the client's brand and goals.

2. E-COMMERCE DEVELOPMENT
   Full online store setup using WooCommerce (WordPress) or Shopify. Includes product
   setup, payment gateway integration (PayPal, Stripe, Authorize.net), shipping integration
   (USPS, UPS), and a clean customer-facing shopping experience.

3. FIGMA TO WORDPRESS / PSD TO HTML CONVERSION
   Have a design ready? Danish can convert any Figma or Adobe XD design, or PSD mockup,
   into a fully functional, pixel-perfect WordPress website.

4. WEBFLOW DEVELOPMENT
   Modern no-code/low-code website development using Webflow for clients who prefer
   a more modern, CMS-driven approach.

5. WEBSITE REDESIGN
   Existing website looking outdated? Danish redesigns and modernizes websites while
   preserving SEO rankings and improving conversion rates.

6. ONLINE BUSINESS CONSULTING
   For entrepreneurs and business owners who need strategy — not just a website.
   Danish advises on digital presence, online business setup, monetization strategies,
   and growth planning.

7. ENTREPRENEURIAL MENTORSHIP
   One-on-one mentorship for people who want to start or grow an online business or
   freelance career. Danish shares practical knowledge from his 15+ years of experience.
